Research

Building trustworthy wireless systems

The ANTS lab focuses on real-world networking and security problems where mobility, scale, and adversarial behavior make conventional approaches insufficient.

Security

Vehicular network security

Sybil attacks, rogue and malicious nodes, intrusion detection, false-information attacks, and resilient communication in VANETs.

Systems

Edge & distributed computing

Fog/edge architectures, distributed detection, efficient routing, and low-latency processing for dynamic wireless environments.

Future wireless

6G, IoT & intelligent transportation

Security and networking foundations for connected vehicles, autonomous transportation, IoT, and next-generation wireless systems.

Research funding

Translating research into impact

Recent funded activities support research on autonomous vehicles, rural and tribal transportation, and research experiences.

$15M

U.S. Department of Transportation

TRAVELS — Tribal & Rural Autonomous Vehicles for Efficiency, Livability and Safety is the passenger transportation component of U.S. DOT’s Rural Autonomous Vehicle Research Program, led by the University of Wisconsin–Madison. The project aims to improve safety, accessibility, and mobility in rural and Tribal communities through automated vehicle demonstrations in Wisconsin, Georgia, and Oklahoma. My work will focus on communication and security for these automated mobility systems.
