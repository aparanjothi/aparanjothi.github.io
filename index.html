Research

Building trustworthy wireless systems

The ANTS lab focuses on real-world networking and security problems where mobility, scale, and adversarial behavior make conventional approaches insufficient.

Security

Vehicular network security

Sybil attacks, rogue and malicious nodes, intrusion detection, false-information attacks, and resilient communication in VANETs.

Systems

Edge & distributed computing

Fog/edge architectures, distributed detection, efficient routing, and low-latency processing for dynamic wireless environments.

Future wireless

6G, IoT & intelligent transportation

Security and networking foundations for connected autonomous vehicles, autonomous transportation, IoT, and next-generation wireless systems.

Adaptive detection · CCWC 2026

Detecting Sybil attacks with dynamic clustering

Research problemFalse vehicle identities can distort traffic information, while a fixed number of clusters may not suit different road scenarios.

Our contributionOur coauthored framework adapts the number of clusters to group vehicle observations and identify Sybil nodes, evaluated using OMNeT++ and SUMO simulations.

Gideon Adele · Anirudh Paranjothi · Mohammad S. Khan

Information integrity · WiMob 2025

Tracing false information with SWIFT

Research problemConnected and autonomous vehicles depend on shared information; false messages can undermine the reliability of cooperative networks.

Our contributionOur coauthored SWIFT framework uses sliding-window evaluation to trace information falsification in connected and autonomous vehicle networks.

Abinash Borah · Anirudh Paranjothi

Edge security · Preprint 2021

Collaborative Sybil detection at the network edge

Research problemDetecting rogue nodes in dense vehicular networks can introduce processing delays, communication overhead, and false alarms.

Our contributionOur coauthored FSDV framework brings vehicle onboard units together as a dynamic fog for Sybil detection, with evaluation in OMNeT++ and SUMO.

Anirudh Paranjothi · Mohammed Atiquzzaman
